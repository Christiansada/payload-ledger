# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome contributors of every background and experience level.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sharing private information without consent, and knowingly misleading claims are not accepted. Do not include sensitive fixture data in discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns through GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools where appropriate; do not publish personal details in an issue. Maintainers should apply these expectations consistently and explain moderation decisions when doing so is safe.
